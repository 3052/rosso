# Movistar Play / Movistar TV (Chile)

## Platform
- Angular-based Telefónica service, now at `movistartv.cl` (migrated from `movistarplay.cl`)
- Geo-restricted to Chile only per Movistar's terms (Section 11, "Territorio")

## Registration Hurdles
- Geo-block: restricted to Chilean IPs
- RUT required: valid Chilean national ID (format `12345678-k`)
- Cédula serial number: required to validate/confirm the RUT
- Payment method: Chilean credit card or Movistar Chile bill
- Email verification: passable from US

## Registration Pathways
- **Movistar Customer:** must be existing Movistar Chile home customer (Internet + TV plan), activate via "Mi Movistar" app or "Sucursal Virtual" portal
- **Non-Customer "Movistar Play Full":** ~USD $13.6/mo, 7-day free trial, still requires RUT, Chilean credit card, and geo-blocked outside Chile

## Workaround Options
- VPN with Chilean server: bypasses IP block on streaming but cannot register without RUT + Chilean payment
- Chilean friend/relative to register and share account (violates ToS)
- Chilean channels (Chilevisión, TVN, Mega, Canal 13) have own streaming/YouTube; premium content (HBO, ESPN) via Max, ESPN+ in US

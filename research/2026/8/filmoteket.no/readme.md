Filmoteket (filmoteket.no) — Norway's public library film streaming service —
cannot be used from the US because account creation requires a Norwegian
national library card linked to the National Borrower Register (or Deichman
Oslo). Obtaining that card requires registering through ID-porten
(MinID/BankID), which in turn requires a Norwegian national ID number
(fødselsnummer or D-nummer) — unavailable to US residents. In-person
registration in Norway for foreigners without a Norwegian ID number results in
a local library card only, which Filmoteket does not accept. The service itself
is not geo-restricted; the sole barrier is the national library card.

## Summary: Why US residents cannot sign up for Danish public library services (login.bib.dk)

**1. No self-service sign-up exists**
`login.bib.dk` is only a login portal for Denmark's national library system. It
accepts an existing CPR number, borrower number, or MitID — there is no
registration option on the site itself.

**2. Registration requires a Danish CPR number**
To become a borrower, you must register with a Danish municipal library, and
all of them require a **CPR number** (Danish civil registration number). CPR
numbers are only issued to people legally residing in Denmark. US residents
cannot obtain one.

**3. Online registration requires MitID**
Individual library sign-up pages (e.g., Albertslund, Allerød) all route
registration through **MitID**, Denmark's national digital ID. MitID is only
issued to holders of a CPR number, so this path is also blocked for US
residents.

**4. In-person registration requires being physically in Denmark**
The alternative is to visit a library in person with a passport and residence permit/visa — which requires travel to Denmark and still depends on residency status.

**5. No foreign-resident option exists**
Danish public libraries offer no remote or international membership tier. The
only exception is the Royal Danish Library (kb.dk), which can register users
without a CPR number — but materials may then only be used **on-site** in
Copenhagen or Aarhus, not remotely.
